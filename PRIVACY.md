# Privacy Policy — RePost for X

_Last updated: November 21, 2026_

RePost for X is a browser extension that helps you repost a photo or video from an X (x.com) post to your own account.

## What the extension accesses

- **Content of X pages you're viewing.** When you click the RePost button on a post, the extension reads that post's text, the author's @handle and its media URLs. It uses them to fill the X post composer in your own tab.
- **Media files from X's CDN** (`pbs.twimg.com`, `video.twimg.com`). These are downloaded into your browser so they can be attached to your new post.
- **Local settings.** The on/off switch and the result of your last repost are stored with `chrome.storage.local` on your device.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.
- It does not send data to any server operated by the developer or a third party. All processing happens locally in your browser.
- It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
- It never posts on your behalf. You always review the post and click **Post** yourself.
